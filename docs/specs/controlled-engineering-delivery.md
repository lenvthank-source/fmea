## Reference and verification limits

The full plan is **not independently standards-validated**. Public references verify methodology/applicability and the specifically recorded IATF interpretations, but do not expose complete authorized S/O/D criteria or the full DFMEA AP lookup. All-1,000 frontend/backend parity is not an independently sourced expected-result test. Do not mark that acceptance criterion passed until an authorized complete reference supplies the expected results. Do not reconstruct tables from memory or unlicensed mirrors.

Public authority and editions:

- [VDA QMC FMEA overview](https://vda-qmc.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/VDA-QMC-White-Paper-FMEA-Englisch.pdf): seven activities and analytical relationships, not complete numerical tables.
- [AIAG/VDA errata, June 2020](https://www.aiag.org/docs/default-source/training-and-resources/erratta-documents/aiag-vda-fmea-handbook-errata-june-2020.pdf), p1: boundary diagram **or** structure tree; function/parameter representations as applicable.
- [AIAG CP-1](https://www.aiag.org/training-and-resources/manuals/details/CP-1): first edition 2024. [AIAG FAQ](https://www.aiag.org/docs/default-source/quality-/apqp_cp-faqs.pdf): public change list is insufficient for complete gap analysis.
- [IATF November 2025 sanctioned interpretations](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/IATF-16949-SIs-Nov-2025.pdf): SI11 p13, SI24 pp22–23, SI25 pp23–24. Applicability and remaining unchanged requirements must be checked against the complete applicable standard.
- [AIAG/Plexus whitepaper](https://www.aiag.org/docs/default-source/quality-/aiag-plexus-apqp-white-paper-2023.pdf), p9: volume/containment and safe-launch/reaction context; no universal sample or duration.

Customer overlays remain unconfigured. FMEA-MSR and reverse synchronization are excluded by the approved scope. Deployment and customer-specific form acceptance remain outside these local implementation checks. Existing 11 legacy sampling candidates need a human engineering decision, not an automatic fix.
