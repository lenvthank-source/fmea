### Fixes
- Preserve tenant/ownership/revision guards on PFD/repository mutations and protect approved downstream metadata.
- Advance topology versions for operation mutations and account for import-owned version increments without overwriting concurrent edits.
- Reject fractional/non-finite ratings, malformed graph data and ambiguous operation mappings; preserve existing AP mappings.
- Do not derive process labels from equipment or silently turn WIP/rejection into storage/rework/scrap. Mixed legacy data stays draft for engineering review.
- Correct public simulator claims and illustrative rating labels; remove automatic risk-acceptance claims.
- Fix narrow-screen action/filter overflow, repository connector encoding and invalid navigation/table HTML nesting; wait for topology load before export.
### Refactors
- Canonical source/rule register distinguishes methodology, customer requirements, product policy and known implementation gaps.
- Track generated source/migration README indexes, ignore runtime logs/temporary locks, and maintain focused handoff plus refreshed Graphify artifacts.
