# v0.8.3 — 2026-10-18

## Summary
Deploys a comprehensive visual overhaul across the public landing page to vividly demonstrate the platform's core mission: retiring fragile, disconnected FMEA Excel sheets in favor of deterministic AIAG-VDA 2019 quality engineering. Replaces the legacy dark risk calculator with a modern light-theme enterprise simulator featuring dynamic side-by-side comparison between the legacy Excel RPN formula and the deterministic AIAG-VDA Action Priority engine. Introduces an interactive animated comparison showcase (`ExcelRetirementShowcase.tsx`) that illustrates the corporate Excel nightmare (formula corruption, #REF! errors, hidden lethal hazards, and orphan steps) contrasted directly against the FMEApex synchronized Golden Thread.

## Changes
### Features & UX Ergonomics
- **Visual "Ditch the FMEA Excel Nightmare" Interactive Showcase**:
  - Implemented `ExcelRetirementShowcase.tsx` mounted in Section 6 of the landing page.
  - Interactive multi-mode viewing:
    1. **Old Excel Sheet**: High-fidelity visual simulation of corporate Excel sheets featuring classic green header (`#107C41`), broken `#REF!` formula errors, unaligned columns, and misleading green RPN scores.
    2. **FMEApex Quality OS**: Clean light enterprise interface showcasing the active Golden Thread (`PFD ↔ PFMEA ↔ Control Plan`), 0 orphan rows, and 21 CFR Part 11 cryptographic signatures.
    3. **Interactive Side-by-Side Split**: Simultaneous side-by-side inspection highlighting 4 fatal Excel vulnerabilities against 4 deterministic FMEApex defenses.
  - Added autoplay cycling with pause/play controls and interactive vulnerability inspection cards.

- **Light Enterprise Action Priority & Risk Simulator Overhaul**:
  - Completely replaced the dark `#0B0F19` console in `RiskCalculatorWidget.tsx` with a modern light enterprise interface matching the FMEApex design system.
  - Integrated a direct **"Legacy Excel RPN Math vs. AIAG-VDA 2019 Decision"** dual breakdown:
    - **Excel RPN Box**: Dynamically computes `RPN = S × O × D` and flags dangerous blindspots (e.g., Severity 9 hazard evaluated to RPN 36 and skipped as "Low Risk") or false panics (cosmetic defect evaluated to RPN 126, forcing unnecessary rework).
    - **AIAG-VDA Decision Box**: Demonstrates deterministic Severity-first evaluation producing **HIGH AP** regardless of low occurrence, triggering mandatory action workflows.
  - Tactile range sliders with colored bullets and live engineering descriptions across Severity [1-10], Occurrence [1-10], and Detection [1-10].
  - 1-click AI Closed-Loop Optimization simulation that demonstrates post-action risk reduction from High AP to Low AP with live before/after rating indicators.
