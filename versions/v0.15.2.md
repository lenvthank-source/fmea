# v0.15.2 — 2026-10-23

## Summary
Fixes runtime 500 `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'filter')` during Control Plan source synchronization preview (`sync-preview`) and decision application (`apply`). Dispatches `previewCp` and `applyCpChanges` to `previewCpV2` and `applyCpChangesV2` when encountering standardized Schema Version 2 (`CpDocumentV2`) documents, and guards V1-specific detached lines cleanup in `ControlPlanDocumentService.apply`.

## Changes
### Fixes
- Fixed Control Plan Sync Preview 500 Crash: `previewCp` and `applyCpChanges` now check `isCpDocumentV2(document)` and route to `previewCpV2` and `applyCpChangesV2` respectively, eliminating `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'filter')` caused by referencing missing `document.lines` on V2 documents.
- Guarded Detached Lines Cleanup on Apply: In `ControlPlanDocumentService.apply`, wrapped the flat line reference check (`document.lines.filter`) in `if (!isCpDocumentV2(document))` to ensure applying source synchronization decisions on V2 documents proceeds cleanly without property errors.
- Unit Test Coverage: Added a dedicated unit test in `control-plan-document.spec.ts` validating that `previewCp` and `applyCpChanges` accurately process candidate lines into structured V2 operations, characteristics, controls, and source bindings.
