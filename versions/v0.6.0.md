# v0.6.0 — 2026-10-17

## Summary
Complete frontend overhaul transforming the marketing, landing, educational, and programmatic SEO experience of FMEApex from generic AI-generated aesthetic ("AI slop") into a handcrafted, industrial-grade quality engineering platform. Grounded the entire platform in the rigorous standards of AIAG-VDA 2019 7-step analysis, 21 CFR Part 11 cryptographic audit trails, and deterministic Action Priority (AP) calculation tables. Scaffolds the complete 7-subsystem Document-Driven AI Agent Architecture (`.agents/` + `AGENTS.md`) and establishes repository root hygiene by purging leaked credentials and consolidating documentation into `docs/`.

## Changes
### Features
- **Industrial Quality Engineering Design System**: Overhauled `frontend/src/index.css` with dark ink (`#0F172A`), precision technical grid patterns (`.bg-tech-grid`, `.bg-tech-dots`), hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`), high-contrast Action Priority badges (`badge-high-ap`, `badge-med-ap`, `badge-low-ap`), and mechanical range sliders (`.fmea-range`).
- **Live Infrastructure Telemetry (AGENTS.md Rule 4.1)**: Implemented `BackendWarmupContainer` in `frontend/src/hooks/useBackendWarmup.ts` and wrapped `App.tsx` around `AppRouter`. Displays live `/health` telemetry (`RLS Active • Live API` with emerald pulse vs `Warming API...` with amber pulse) in `SiteHeader` and manages cold-start states on sandbox CTA buttons.
- **Unified Navigation & Solutions Mega-Menu**: Upgraded `SiteHeader.tsx` and `SiteFooter.tsx` with smooth-scrolling anchors (`#workflow`, `#simulator`, `#sync`, `#specs`), a categorized solutions dropdown (Automotive IATF 16949, Aerospace AS9100, Medical ISO 13485, Semiconductor JEDEC), and an exhaustive footer link matrix spanning methodologies, compliance, competitors, and glossaries.
- **Interactive Landing Workspaces & 7-Step Method Explorer**: Completely redesigned `LandingPage.tsx` with step-gating criteria, a "Spreadsheet Chaos vs. Deterministic Rigor" comparison matrix, enterprise architectural specs, and a tactile risk simulator (`RiskCalculatorWidget.tsx`) with AIAG-VDA 2019 AP calculation.
- **Full Programmatic SEO & Content Pages Integration**:
  - `IndustryFmeaPage.tsx`: Integrated `SiteHeader`, `SiteFooter`, technical breadcrumbs, industry tab navigator, AIAG-VDA failure chain matrix, and Princeton-GEO regulatory blocks.
  - `CompetitorVsPage.tsx`: Added `SiteHeader`, `SiteFooter`, competitor switcher tabs, and a high-contrast architectural comparison matrix (FMEApex vs Excel / APIS IQ-RM).
  - `GlossaryPage.tsx`: Integrated header/footer, structured definition blocks, and engineering cross-references.
  - `PillarPage.tsx`: Purged rotated "FREE" sticker badges and conflicting color palettes, aligning with industrial engineering tokens.
  - `BlogListPage.tsx` & `BlogPostPage.tsx`: Converted into an authoritative engineering journal with tag filtering and reading time metrics.
- **Agentic Architecture Scaffolding**: Implemented the full 7-subsystem Document-Driven AI Agent Architecture under `.agents/` (`AGENTS.md`, `rules/`, `agents/`, `memory/`, `skills/`, `workflows/`, `checklists/`, `context/`, `scripts/dispatch.py`).

### Fixes
- **TypeScript TS6133 Compilation Errors**: Cleaned unused imports and variables in `frontend/src/components/site/FmeaStudioPreview.tsx` to restore production build integrity.
- **Secret Hygiene & Root Restructuring**: Purged `FMEA Credentials.txt` containing live plaintext database credentials from repository root. Created sanitized template `backend/.env.example`.
- **Duplicate & Corrupted Asset Cleanup**: Consolidated legacy and root design assets into `docs/assets/`, deleting duplicate root `Pictures/` and legacy icons.
- **Gitignore Parent Exclusion Bug**: Fixed `.gitignore` to track all `.agents/` rules, personas, memory specifications, and `docs/`.
