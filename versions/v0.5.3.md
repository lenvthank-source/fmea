# v0.5.3 — 2026-09-01
## Summary
Resolves layout and CSS cascade issues by enforcing Tailwind v4 @layer boundaries and StyledEngineProvider injection, completely rebuilds the public Home Page and marketing pages into the Ventriloc luxury aesthetic (warm ivory canvas, modular tiles, 3D tilt interactive dashboard, and translucent fluid glass header with slap cap FREE badge), overhauls the brand favicon, and equips the platform with full Google and AI search discovery assets (FAQPage schema, modern robots.txt bot rules, llms.txt, and llms-full.txt).

## Changes
### Features
- **Translucent Fluid Glass Header:** `frontend/src/components/site/SiteHeader.tsx:1` single floating capsule navbar with `backdrop-blur-xl`, border sheen, living fluid motion streams (orange, gold, and amber gradient blobs drifting with `@keyframes drift`), and slap cap `FREE` badge perched at an angle on the `Launch Sandbox` button; removed cluttered login and language buttons.
- **Interactive 3D Tilt Dashboard Showcase:** `frontend/src/components/site/VentrilocDashboard.tsx:1` converted from dark container to an interactive light-themed application studio with mouse-tracked 3D tilt (±3°), cursor radial glow, animated station risk progress bars, and lift-on-hover Bento cards.
- **Ventriloc Modular Tiles Architecture:** `frontend/src/features/landing/LandingPage.tsx:1` modular tile layout: Editorial Hero, The Studio Showcase, Partner Marquee, 4 Light Core Quality Modules (Module 03 converted from dark to warm ivory), Interactive Risk Simulator, Technical Architecture Bento, and Transformation Contact form.
- **Public Marketing Pages Theme Alignment:** Upgraded `ProductPage.tsx:1`, `PricingPage.tsx:1`, `AboutPage.tsx:1`, and `LearnHubPage.tsx:1` from legacy teal/slate to the warm ivory `#FAF9F6` palette with Plus Jakarta Sans typography, high-impact cards, and sandbox conversion CTAs.
- **Brand Favicon Redesign:** `frontend/public/favicon.svg:1` modern dark graphite squircle (`#141416`) with crisp white geometric `F` lettermark and electric orange `#FF682C` accent slash with soft glow.
- **Search & AI Discovery Integration (AI SEO):**
  - `frontend/index.html:1` rich JSON-LD graph integrating `Organization`, `WebSite`, `SoftwareApplication`, and `FAQPage` schema with 5 direct-answer blocks for Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT Search.
  - `frontend/public/robots.txt:1` permissions for `GPTBot`, `ChatGPT-User`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `anthropic-ai`, `Google-Extended`, `Applebot-Extended`, and `Meta-ExternalAgent`.
  - `frontend/public/llms.txt:1` & `frontend/public/llms-full.txt:1` machine-readable standard context documents with Princeton GEO statistics, direct answer definitions, and architecture specifications.

### Fixes
- **CSS Cascade Layer Overrides:** `frontend/src/index.css:1` wrapped custom styles in `@layer base` and `@layer components`, eliminating an unlayered wildcard `* { margin: 0; padding: 0 }` reset that was defeating Tailwind v4 utility classes (`mx-auto`, `p-8`, `text-center`, etc.).
- **Tailwind v4 @import Priority:** `frontend/src/index.css:1` moved `@import "tailwindcss"` to line 1 and migrated Google Webfonts to `<link>` tags in `index.html`.
- **MUI Specificity Inversion:** `frontend/src/theme/ColorModeContext.tsx:150` wrapped MUI theme tree with `<StyledEngineProvider injectFirst>` so Tailwind classes win on specificity ties over Emotion baseline resets.
- **Admin Routing & Permission Loop:** `frontend/src/components/RequirePermission.tsx:1` and `frontend/src/app/router.tsx:52` eliminated asynchronous redirect flicker that was ejecting admin users back to the home page.
